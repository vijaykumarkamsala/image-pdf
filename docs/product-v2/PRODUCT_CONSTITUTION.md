# Product Constitution — Intelligent Visual Production Workspace

## 1. Product promise

Provide one intelligent visual-production workspace where any person or team can take an imperfect idea, image, document or PDF and turn it into a polished, editable, traceable and purpose-ready result.

The product is not an image-to-PDF converter and is not a collection of disconnected one-operation websites. It combines editing, restoration, design, document creation, PDF management and production preparation around one project and asset foundation.

## 2. Product identity

- Independent product and brand.
- Individuals and organisations use the same universal workspace model.
- It may later be accessible under a YearShift umbrella through stable APIs, shared identity or shared billing.
- It must not initially depend on YearShift authentication, database, billing or internal services.
- Its processing services and public API remain reusable by other products.

## 3. Equal parent outcomes

The home page presents major outcomes equally rather than forcing a single document-scanning story:

1. **Image & Graphic Studio**
2. **Create PDF**
3. **Edit & Manage PDF**
4. **Print & Production**

Outcome shortcuts such as Enhance Image, Images to PDF, Merge PDF, Compress PDF, Scan to Searchable PDF and Send for Signature open a focused view inside the appropriate shared workspace. They are not independent mini-applications.

## 4. Customer-centred experience

- Guest home: upload is primary; cloud import and blank creation are secondary; major outcomes remain equally visible.
- Signed-in home: recent projects, default files, quick create/import, shared work and background-job status dominate.
- Beginners receive recommended outcomes and simple language.
- Advanced controls remain complete and discoverable.
- The system explains what it detected, why it recommends a change and whether AI may create detail.
- A high-quality source can proceed without enhancement.
- The product must confidently say “no enhancement required” when that is the honest answer.

## 5. Non-negotiable content principles

- Originals are immutable.
- Every derivative has traceable lineage, recipe, processor/model version and approval state.
- Normal edits are non-destructive adjustments, masks or editable objects.
- AI-generated results become named variants/layers; previous results remain available.
- Standard correction never silently invokes generative reconstruction.
- AI-created or substantially reconstructed regions are visible and reviewable.
- Recreate mode always requires explicit permission.
- Text, logos, facial identity, signatures and critical outlines receive protection appropriate to the use case.
- “More pixels” is not presented as “more recovered truth.”
- Production-ready is a measurable status for a selected use, size and profile—not a marketing label.

## 6. Intelligent intake

Before changing pixels or PDF objects, the application:

- Validates the real file type and safety.
- Preserves the original and creates safe previews.
- Reads dimensions, profiles, bit depth, transparency, orientation, pages/frames and metadata.
- Detects content type and meaningful regions.
- Detects defects and source limitations.
- Infers intended use and asks only when the answer materially changes processing.
- Generates a proposed preview, but applies nothing without confirmation.
- Removes sensitive GPS/device metadata from exports by default while allowing an explicit preserve option.

## 7. Enhancement truth model

The system distinguishes:

| Mode | Intended use | Detail policy |
|---|---|---|
| Preserve | Medical/legal documents, logos, exact artwork | Does not intentionally invent content |
| Restore | Photos and illustrations with recoverable degradation | Cautious reconstruction, disclosed |
| Recreate | Severely limited sources requiring plausible new detail | Explicitly generative and approved |

The safest applicable mode is recommended automatically. When the desired physical output exceeds trustworthy source capacity, the system shows the largest trustworthy result and offers Recreate separately.

## 8. Unified editor foundation

- Shared canvas, layers, assets, history, comments, save and export concepts across image/design/PDF work.
- Clean docked panel layout by default; panels may detach, float, pin, resize and be saved as workspace presets.
- Dockable conversational AI assistant plus contextual AI actions on selected objects/regions.
- Raster images, vectors, text and shapes coexist as independent editable layers.
- Multiple artboards can have different sizes while sharing assets and styles.
- Smart Resize creates a new artboard and proposes rearrangement; it never destroys the original.
- Continuous autosave, persistent undo/redo, automatic checkpoints and named versions.
- Desktop provides the full professional environment; tablet is near-full; phone prioritises view, comment, export and essential quick edits.

## 9. Projects and workspaces

- Every account receives a personal workspace and a default project/files area.
- The same model supports individuals, companies and informal teams.
- Customers may use a lightweight asset-oriented project or a full structured project.
- Collections and true subprojects are both supported.
- Workspace, collection, project, subproject, document and version permissions can inherit and be overridden transparently.
- Original files, versions, comments, approvals, jobs and exports belong to workspace-controlled records.

## 10. Collaboration

- Only one editor holds a renewable lock on a document at a time.
- Other users may view/comment on that document and edit different documents in the same project.
- Lock expiry autosaves work; owners may request or force takeover with warning and audit.
- Comments anchor to exact regions and versions; unresolved threads carry forward with traceability.
- Formal approval is optional and configured per workspace/project.
- Sharing supports signed-in collaborators and controlled anonymous view/comment/download links.
- Editing requires an authenticated collaborator.
- Share links can target a live project/result or a fixed snapshot and support expiry, password, download policy and revocation.

## 11. Output equality

Digital and physical outputs receive equal product importance.

Digital profiles cover web, apps, email, social, marketplaces, presentations, archives and custom sizes.

Physical profiles cover paper/card, posters/signage, DTG/DTF, screen print, sublimation, direct-to-fabric, seamless repeats, embroidery preparation and cut/vector production.

One editable master may produce many independently preflighted derivatives. Physical proof tracking is optional and appears only when enabled by a project or production profile.

## 12. Commercial launch posture

- The application is free during testing.
- Customers are not charged and do not manage payment methods, wallets or credits.
- A production-grade usage ledger and versioned pricing engine operate in shadow mode.
- Experimental prices remain admin-only.
- Fair-use and technical safety limits protect compute and abuse; they are not billing limits.
- Testers see understandable non-monetary usage.

## 13. Security and privacy constitution

- Customer content is private by default and is never used for training by default.
- Training requires a separately contributed copy, verified rights and explicit revocable consent.
- Support access is user-approved, time-limited, purpose-bound and audited; break-glass is tightly controlled.
- Workspace home region controls storage/processing where supported; exceptions are disclosed.
- Customer credentials, passwords, signing secrets and files are never written to ordinary logs.
- Dangerous PDF active content is disabled in sandboxed safe mode.
- Deletion revokes access immediately, uses a disclosed trash period and then purges active storage and expires backups within a documented window.

## 14. Release integrity

The product does not release to external testers with known Critical/High defects, blocked approved journeys or unowned Medium defects without accepted workarounds. Rollout proceeds through internal QA, trusted/domain users and closed beta with feature flags and rollback.

## 15. Explicit exclusions

- Video editor
- Live charging/payment collection before pricing approval
- Unlicensed fonts, models or proprietary content
- Password bypass for protected PDFs
- A promise of perfect arbitrary PDF-to-Office conversion
- A promise that every image can become infinitely zoomable or truthfully recover missing detail
- Tight coupling to YearShift
